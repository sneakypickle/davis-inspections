Sewer Scope Camera Inspections

A standard home inspection evaluates the visible interior plumbing drains and fixtures inside the home, but the underground main sewer lateral running from the foundation out to the city sewer tap (or septic tank) is buried several feet beneath the yard and street.

Common Sewer Line Issues in Colorado

  • Tree Root Intrusion: Roots enter joints in clay, concrete, or cast-iron pipes and cause backups.
  • Pipe Bellies & Settling: Expansive Colorado soils can shift, creating low spots that trap waste and water.
  • Cracked, Collapsed, or Orangeburg Pipe: Older or damaged pipe materials that require excavation to repair.
  • Separated Joints & Tap Connections: Misaligned connections where the home's line meets the municipal main.

Continuous 48-Hour Radon Testing

$125 + applicable trip fees

Radon is a colorless, odorless, radioactive gas produced by the natural breakdown of uranium in soil and rock. The entire Colorado Front Range—including El Paso County—is classified by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) as Zone 1 (Highest Radon Potential), where a high percentage of homes test above the EPA action level of 4.0 pCi/L.

Professional Continuous Electronic Monitoring

  • NRSB Certified Specialist: Matt Davis is certified by the National Radon Safety Board (NRSB #23MS022; Davis Home Inspections NRSB #1156).
  • Airthings Corentium Pro Monitors: Lab-calibrated continuous monitors record hourly radon, temperature, humidity, and barometric pressure readings over a 48-hour period.
  • Fast Results: No waiting weeks for mail-in charcoal canisters—your detailed hourly graph report is generated right after pickup.

Closed-Building Protocol Requirements

To ensure a valid, EPA-compliant 48-hour radon measurement:

  • All windows and exterior doors must remain closed (except for normal entry and exit) starting 12 hours prior to test placement and throughout the 48-hour test.
  • Whole-house fans and evaporative (swamp) coolers must not be operated during the test.

Termite & Wood-Destroying Insect (WDI) Inspections

$125 Includes Official NPMA-33 Report

Subterranean termites, carpenter ants, carpenter bees, and wood-boring beetles can cause hidden structural damage to framing, sill plates, subfloors, and exterior wood trim. In addition, VA mortgage loans (and certain FHA/lender underwriters) require a formal Wood-Destroying Insect (WDI) inspection documented on the federal NPMA-33 form prior to closing.

What the WDI Inspection Covers

  • Visual inspection, probing, and sounding of accessible structural wood in basements, crawlspaces, garages, attics, porches, and exterior perimeters.
  • Identification of live insects, dead insects, mud shelter tubes, frass, exit holes, or wood damage.
  • Documentation of any evidence of prior pest control treatments.

Official NPMA-33 Documentation for VA Buyers
